# Visual rework: a realistic stadium, code only

Goal: the look of the owner's concept images (a floodlit night stadium in a harbour, wet striped
grass, a steel cage with wire netting, floodlight towers, stands with a crowd, banners and LED
boards, a city and harbour behind; and the same stadium in five other settings), with **no asset
files** (every mesh, texture and sound is generated by this repository's code) and **without making
the game slow**.

It is based on a measured prototype (a throwaway lab outside the repo, 2026-10-20). That study scored
closeness to the concept at 12/100 for the game before this work and 48/100 for the first prototype.
It estimates about 70/100 is reachable on the high tier (about 3 ms of GPU at 1600x900 on a Turing
desktop GPU; the game used 1.2 ms) and about 60/100 on the low tier (about 1.2 ms).

## Quality tiers (`src/render/quality.ts`)

Three tiers, one table of switches (`TIERS`) that every view reads:

| | low (Performance) | high (default on desktop) | ultra (opt-in) |
|---|---|---|---|
| AA | FXAA | MSAA x4 | MSAA x4 |
| bloom | none (glow from geometry) | emissive bloom | emissive bloom |
| resolution | pixel ratio 1 | pixel budget + dynamic resolution | bigger budget + dynamic resolution |
| shadows | 1 key, 2048 | 2 keys, 2048 | 2 keys, 4096 |
| turf | flat filtered, damp sheen | relief, puddles, streaks | + grass blades near the camera |
| post | grade, vignette | grade, vignette | + GTAO, SSR on wet turf, lens glare |
| stadium | lighter crowd, no fine netting | full | + floodlight beams |

Phones and tablets start on low, desktops on high, the WebGL2 fallback on low unless the player
chose a tier. Saves from before stored 0 (high) or 1 (performance).

## Themes (`src/render/theme.ts`)

A theme is data: the same generators and shaders read it. Shared facts live in `theme.ts`: the two key
lights, fill, floodlights, wetness, fog, tone mapping and grade. Each view keeps its own per-theme
parameters next to its code, keyed by `ThemeId`. The light count and material variants never change
between themes, so a theme switch only re-bakes and rewrites uniforms; it never recompiles shaders.
Gameplay never reads a theme.

Themes: `harbour-night` (the default), `harbour-day`, `canyon-sunset`, `city-dusk`, `alpine-day`,
`railyard-night`. The Friendly screen's Stadium row picks one (saved with the prefs); a theme not
built yet shows the night harbour.

## Module layout

| folder | owns | built from |
|---|---|---|
| `src/render/world/` | sky (gradient, clouds, stars, moon or sun), backdrop generators (harbour, canyon...), the bake of both into a cube map + PMREM environment, cached in IndexedDB | the old sky dome + environment |
| `src/render/turf/` | pitch material (blade texture baked at load, stripes, wear, wetness, puddles, floodlight pools and streaks, relief), grass blades (ultra) | the old floor material |
| `src/render/stadium/` | cage (steel arches, hex wire netting on the shell), LED boards, banners, stands and crowd, floodlight towers and lamp heads, beams (ultra) | the old truss, cones, stands, masts, halos, lamp bars, glass |
| `src/render/post/` | render pipeline per tier (bloom, grade, vignette, FXAA, GTAO / SSR / flare on ultra), key lights and shadows | the old `applyQuality` chain |
| `Car.ts`, `Ball.ts` | paint with clearcoat flakes, tyres, glass, the panelled ball | same files |

Goals, goal frames and boost pads stay in `Arena.ts`. The collision mesh (`src/sim/arenaMesh.ts`) does not
change: everything drawn on the walls coincides with the shell or sits outside it, and the roof stays
flat because the physics ceiling is flat.

## Contracts every module keeps

- WebGPU and the automatic WebGL2 fallback (TSL only; compute needs a fallback path).
- Glow: bloom reads only the `emissive` MRT target. Every unlit or transparent material sets
  `mrtNode = glowMrt(...)` or `noGlowMrt()` (`materials.ts`). On low there is no bloom, so glow must
  come from geometry and HDR colour there.
- NaN hygiene: saturate every uv, fade and `pow` base. Verify with `#nancheck`.
- The scene renders through the pipeline's PassNode only; tone mapping and sRGB are applied once in
  `renderOutput`.
- No allocation per frame. Static work is baked at load behind the loading overlay. Hidden meshes
  get a warm-up hook (`Game.setWarm`).
- Draw calls: at most 40 on low and 60 on high. One draw per module element is the house style
  (instance or merge).
- Gameplay readability: markings, goal frames, boost pads, ball marker and car silhouettes stay
  legible. Team colours keep their colour-blind separation.
- Original content only. No commercial game names, logos, car designs or layouts. Board and banner
  text is invented and drawn with code.

## Phases

Status (2026-10-21): phases 1-3 are done on `feat/visual-rework`; phase 4 (the other themes) is
being built; phase 5 is open.

1. **Foundation** (no visual change).
   - The three tiers, prefs migration and the menu.
   - Dynamic resolution.
   - Theme plumbing.
   - Move the old sky, environment, floor, stadium and pipeline code into the module folders above,
     as the seams for phase 2.
2. **Modules** (in parallel, each in its folder, ported from the prototype with the critic's fixes).
   - World: sky and backdrop bake.
   - Turf.
   - Stadium.
   - Post and lighting: two shadowed keys, grade per theme, hot lamp heads with tight glare, and the
     ultra extras.
   - Car and ball materials.
3. **Integration and tuning.** Done:
   - The integrated captures' faults: the ultra lens glare keys on white-hot emissive only (no red
     haze from the goal frame); the wet floodlight reflections are soft anisotropic streaks toward
     the camera; the whitish fog on the damp turf (the keys' GGX highlight and the probe's hot lamp
     panels on the sheen) is gone; the dark band across the near pitch (a mowing stripe) is soft;
     round the goals the cage is netting and the goal net draws from both sides (no black box).
   - Toward the concept: the floodlight pools shape the key lights and sit under their shadows
     (car shadows read), a dominant night key, a warmer olive turf, a brighter blue sky, a lit and
     less saturated crowd, fainter goal-mouth glow, a softer vignette.
   - High's shadow maps are 2048 (high 163 MB of textures instead of 372); memory is flat over
     tier and theme switches; the ball and car blobs float above the ultra grass blades.
   - The WebGL2 fallback starts on low unless the player chose a tier; `#webgl2` forces it for
     testing. Both backends compile the boot's pipelines in parallel (Renderer.precompile): the
     first frame on WebGPU high is ready in ~2.7 s instead of ~5.9 s. Boot marks in main.ts.
   - The Friendly screen's Stadium row (the theme picker of phase 4).
   - Measured: see "Measuring" below and ARCHITECTURE.md "Rendering budget".
4. **The other five themes** (the menu row to pick one exists since phase 3).
5. **Review, docs** (`ARCHITECTURE.md`), changelog, and the owner's approval before the deploy.

## Measuring

- `#stats` in the URL: GPU ms (timestamp queries) and draw calls in the F3 line.
- Compare tiers at the chase camera on the same machine.
- Report: GPU ms per tier at 1600x900 and at the screen's size, draw calls, triangles, load time
  (bake and first-frame compile).

Targets on the reference Turing GPU at 1600x900:

| tier | GPU | draw calls |
|---|---|---|
| low | ≤ 1.5 ms | ≤ 40 |
| high | ≤ 3.5 ms | ≤ 60 |
| ultra | ≤ 5 ms | — |

First load ≤ 4 s on WebGPU high with a warm cache.

Phase 3 results (free play at kickoff, chase camera, median of three 120-frame means, the GPU
shared with other sessions):

| tier | GPU | draw calls | triangles | textures |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| low | 0.84 ms | 28 | 90k | 69 MB |
| high | 1.99 ms | 42 | 136k | 163 MB |
| ultra | 3.18 ms | 53 | 371k | 377 MB |

Open against the targets:
- A 3v3 match frame draws 51 calls on low and 80 on high (each car: body, wheels, and both again
  per shadowed key), and ultra takes 5.97 ms there. Options: instance every car body and every
  wheel set into one draw each, or a single shadowed key for the bots' cars.
- First load (production build, warm world cache, WebGPU high): 2.3-2.9 s to the first frame
  finished on the GPU (median ~2.7 s; 2.2 s on low), within the 4 s target since the boot compiles
  its pipelines in parallel; a contended GPU process stretched single runs to ~9 s. A tier switch
  still compiles synchronously (1-3 s on the GPU). Shader sizes: turf 63 KB, car 60 KB, ball
  52 KB, four lit stadium materials 30-34 KB each.
