# Code of Conduct

Turbogol is built by people from different countries, languages and skill levels, in the game and
in the code. We want this to be a welcoming place for all of them.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) and applies
to every project space: issues, pull requests, Discussions, chats and online matches organised by
the project.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language.
- Give and take constructive feedback about the work, never about the person.
- Assume good intent; ask before concluding.
- Credit other people's work and respect licenses.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory jokes and language of any kind.
- Sexualised content, doxxing (publishing someone's private information), or stalking.
- Cheating tools, exploits or griefing shared in project spaces to harm other players.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or encouraging any of the above.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers privately (see
`SECURITY.md` for the private reporting channel, or the contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile).
Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone
who breaks this code, depending on the severity and on whether it happened before. Maintainers who
do not follow it may be removed from their role.
